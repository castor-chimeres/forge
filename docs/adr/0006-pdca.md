# ADR-0006 — PDCA of agentic practice

**Status:** Accepted (M0)

## Decision
Use Plan-Do-Check-Act to improve the **practice of agentic engineering**: roles, governance, tools, workflow, quality and economics. PDCA is not a mandatory prompt-writing or SDD recipe.

## Consequences
Capture comparable baseline measurements, failures and human observations. Review hypotheses before changing policies or promoting practices.
